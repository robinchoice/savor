Give me a morning briefing for this project.

1. Summarize the commits of the last 24 hours (or since the last weekday) in plain language: what changed for users, what changed under the hood.
2. Run the project's checks (tests, typecheck, build, whatever exists) and report what fails, with the likely cause in one sentence each.
3. Look for open threads: TODO and FIXME comments added recently, failing or skipped tests, half-finished work on the current branch.
4. Suggest three concrete things to do today, smallest first.

Keep it under 200 words. Do not change any files.
