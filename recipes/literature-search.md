---
Search the literature for the question or chapter I name in this run. Without one, take the chapter in manuscript/ that has the fewest citations for its length.

Search OpenAlex, Crossref and Semantic Scholar. Keep a paper only if its DOI resolves at doi.org and title, authors and year match. Never list a source you have not found there.

For each paper you keep: add an entry to references.bib with a readable citekey, unless Zotero writes that file (see PROJECT.md); then list the DOIs for me to import. Write a note in notes/literature/ with the citekey, what the paper claims, its method and sample, and the page or section the claim is on.

Conclude with the five most relevant papers, one sentence each on why they matter for the chapter.
